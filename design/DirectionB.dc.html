// a podcast on software and AI

Where AI meets the reality of software development.

AI is reshaping how we build software products. How are teams actually adapting?

Arthur Magne and Yannick Grenzinger talk with the people living this transformation day to day — the practices, the organisational choices, the wins and the struggles.
