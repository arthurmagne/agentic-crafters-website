What we ask every guest

How your teams actually use AI
Beyond the demos and the vendor decks. What people reach for on a Tuesday afternoon.
What it changes in how you organise
Roles, rituals, the place of junior developers, who decides what.
What happens to code quality
Review, tests, and the debt that does not show up for another two quarters.
What actually reaches production
Delivered value, not generated lines. And how fast it gets there.
What you measure
The numbers teams trust, and everything that still resists measurement.
